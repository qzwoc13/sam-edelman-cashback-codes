Why do codes fail so often? The documented reasons are worth skimming, because most are avoidable:

- The code expired or the store switched it off after publication
- It was single-use and someone already redeemed it
- It only covers certain categories or individual products
- Your cart didn't hit the minimum spend
- It's limited to new customers or an email sign-up
- **It can't be combined with an active sale or another code** — this one bites the most, since a code that failed on a marked-down item may work fine on full price
